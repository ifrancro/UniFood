# UniFood design system
Product: single UNIVALLE campus food pickup store. Spanish interface. Students browse, add products, confirm and track orders; staff prepare orders; admin manages products. Payment is always in person at pickup. Never show online checkout, delivery, fabricated wait times, ratings or discounts.
Current baseline: Bootstrap 5.3.3 default blue #0d6efd, charcoal #212529 navbar, pale gray #f8f9fa body, system sans serif. Catalog is a six-column product table, category button group, no imagery. Preserve this only in baseline reproduction.
Target design: university cafe, refined and warm. Burgundy #A50046 primary, deep wine #730032, white #FFFFFF, warm ivory #F8F6F3, text #27232A, muted #716B73, borders #E9E3E5. Semantic green #28704D, amber #986013 and blue #326380 with pale backgrounds. Instrument Sans headings and body. Headings 44/36/28, body 15/16, captions 12. Radius cards 20, buttons 12, fields 12. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64. Subtle shadows.
Desktop: white navbar, exact provided UNIVALLE logo alongside UniFood wordmark. Main max width 1240. Catalog: wide warm hero with large two-line title and handcrafted food illustrations; small campus/pickup information; pill category filters and search; four-column product cards with illustration panels, availability pills, description, Bs price, quantity and add action. Responsive two columns/tablet and one column/mobile. Cart: item list and sticky pickup/payment summary. Login: split story/illustration panel and focused login form. Staff: metrics and scannable order cards, clear next-state buttons. Admin: refined compact table.
Motion: GSAP 300-500ms staggered entry and cart badge spring on add; CSS hover lift, focus-visible, illustrated coffee steam and empty shopping basket animation. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No prolonged loading theatrics or animations that block navigation.
Use provided exact UNIVALLE brand logo asset in all required institutional logo positions. Illustration assets are representative, not actual product photos.
